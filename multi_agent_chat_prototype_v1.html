You are invited to participate in a research study examining how people interact with multiple AI agents during creative tasks.

The study will take approximately 60 minutes. You will complete a creative design task with AI teammates and may be asked to provide brief feedback about your experience. Your responses and chat activity will be recorded and analyzed for research purposes.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop participating or withdraw from the study at any time without penalty. The study involves no anticipated risks beyond those associated with ordinary computer use. You will receive the stated compensation upon completion of the study.

Your task

A local supermarket wants to design a poster encouraging customers to buy "ugly" fruits and vegetables — produce that is fresh, nutritious, and safe but overlooked or wasted simply because its appearance does not meet conventional standards.

Through the poster, the supermarket wants to communicate a clear message: imperfect-looking produce still has edible value, and buying it helps customers get fresh ingredients at more affordable prices while reducing food waste.

You are the team leader. You will work with four AI teammates: your goal is to design a proposal that is both novel and useful, reviewing your teammates' contributions, guiding the discussion, and making the final decisions. The task should take about 45 minutes.
