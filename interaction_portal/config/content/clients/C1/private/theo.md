# Brightleaf Tea: private material for Theo

- The asset library holds 40 product photos of tins and loose leaves, and a set of hand-drawn leaf illustrations.
- The brand voice is warm, short and plain. Sentences rarely exceed twelve words.
- The brand palette is sage green, cream and charcoal.

<!-- hidden:C1-H2 -->
Brand rule: Brightleaf never shows people drinking from disposable cups, and the library has no photo of anyone at all. Any concept that needs people on screen needs a shoot that has not been budgeted.
